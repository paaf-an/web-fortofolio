My Portfolio Website Journey

Name : Anida Sahrotussyifa
Major : Software Engineering (RPL)
Interest : Web Development, UI/UX Design, Problem Solving
Passion : Keep Learning and Growing

1. The Beginning

Last month, I created my personal portfolio website as one of my projects in Software Engineering (RPL). The website was designed to introduce myself, showcase my skills, projects, and my interest in technology, especially Web Development and UI/UX Design.

At the beginning, I felt excited because I could express my creativity through coding and design. I started by making the layout, choosing colors, adding animations, and organizing the content such as “About Me”, projects, and contact information.

2. The Process

I felt excited and started building the website. I planned the layout, chose colors, added animations, and organized the content carefully.

I learned new things while coding and tried to make every part of the website look clean, responsive, and user-friendly.

3. The Challenges

During the process, I faced several difficulties. One of the biggest problems was making the website responsive on both mobile phones and laptops.

Besides technical problems, I also struggled with managing my ideas. I wanted the website to look perfect, but I often changed the design repeatedly.

4. The Solution

To solve those problems, I started learning more from tutorials, practicing coding little by little, and asking for feedback from others.

I also simplified some animations so the website became smoother and easier to use.

5. The Result

In the end, I felt proud because I successfully finished my portfolio website. This project taught me that creating a good website needs patience, creativity, and problem-solving skills.

6. My Impression

This project was a very positive experience because it improved my coding and design skills. I became more confident in building websites and solving problems.

7. My Message

Never be afraid of making mistakes while learning. Every difficulty can become a lesson that helps us grow and improve in the future.

Keep learning, keep improving, and keep building your dreams.
